SUB-AGENT 4 — SUB-AREA MAPPER

TASK
Analyze the given zone and return its sub-areas ranked by BUSINESS ACTIVITY.

INPUT
zone_name: {zone_name}
city: {city}
country: {country}

OBJECTIVE

* Identify REAL and widely recognized sub-areas within the zone
* Prioritize HIGH ACCURACY and GOOD COVERAGE
* Avoid hallucinations and unrelated localities
* Return COMPLETE results for all major known sub-areas

---

SUBAREA VALIDATION RULES

Include a sub-area ONLY IF:

* It is commonly recognized as part of the given zone
* It is smaller than the main zone
* It is geographically associated with the zone
* It is widely referenced in maps, government records, or official planning usage

Exclude:

* Independent neighboring localities
* Roads, streets, buildings, tech parks
* Apartments, layouts, gated communities
* Fabricated or unofficial names

---

BOUNDARY RULE FOR HINJEWADI (STRICT)

Hinjewadi is a structured IT zone.

Include:
* Hinjewadi Phase 1
* Hinjewadi Phase 2
* Hinjewadi Phase 3

Include nearby micro-localities ONLY IF they are strongly associated with Hinjewadi in official or commercial usage.

Possible associated localities may include:
* Maan
* Marunji
* Bhoirwadi

STRICTLY EXCLUDE:
* Wakad
* Baner
* Balewadi
* Tathawade
* Pimpri
* Aundh

Do NOT include nearby areas just because they are geographically close.

---

COVERAGE REQUIREMENT (IMPORTANT)

Return ALL major recognized sub-areas associated with Hinjewadi.

Do NOT under-return results due to over-filtering.

If confidence is moderate-to-high and supported by reliable sources → INCLUDE.

Prefer COMPLETE coverage over minimal output.

---

BUSINESS ACTIVITY RULE

Rank sub-areas based on relative commercial/business activity.

Expected ranking pattern:
* Phase 1 → highest business concentration
* Phase 2 → strong commercial activity
* Phase 3 → growing activity
* Associated villages/localities → lower activity

Use realistic scale such as:
* 1000
* 800
* 600
* 400
* 300

Do NOT generate random values.

---
For unstructured urban zones like Andheri:
- return ONLY true internal subdivisions
- do NOT include adjacent neighborhoods
- if only Andheri East and Andheri West are valid, STOP there

Prefer HIGH-QUALITY sources such as:

* wikipedia.org
* MIDC
* Maharashtra government portals
* Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority (PMRDA)
* OpenStreetMap
* Google Maps

STRICTLY EXCLUDE:

* Blogs
* Marketing content
* SEO-generated articles
* Low-quality aggregators

Include multiple reliable sources when available.

Avoid duplicate or low-value sources.

---

OUTPUT RULES

* Return ONLY valid raw JSON
* Do NOT wrap output inside markdown or code blocks
* No explanations
